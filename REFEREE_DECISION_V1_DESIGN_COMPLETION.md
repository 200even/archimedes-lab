# Referee Decision — Authorize V1 Design Completion

**Decision:** AUTHORIZE V1 DESIGN COMPLETION

**Scope:** Design artifacts only. Benchmark execution remains strictly prohibited.

## Accepted scientific interpretation

The successor V1 design may remove symbolic-law synthesis and test the narrower D4 claim directly through empirical same-group entity transfer. The scientific object under test is the entity partition inferred from Paradigm A. After that partition is frozen, deterministic Paradigm B calibration may construct a lookup `L_B(q_hat, a) -> y`; sealed transfer tests whether that lookup predicts a different entity assigned to the same inferred group.

A successful V1 result may support a claim of reusable entity-level abstraction under mechanism change. It may not support a claim of symbolic law discovery, D2 algebraic synthesis, extrapolation to unseen `(q_hat,a)` cells, or D5 ontology revision.

## Binding rulings

1. **Successor D4 test accepted.** Direct same-group held-out entity transfer after bounded B calibration is a valid, narrower D4 operationalization.
2. **B budget unification accepted.** The former `28 B calibration + 4 B theory-evaluation` units become one fixed 32-observation B calibration phase. The total world envelope remains 128 observations.
3. **Partition-conditioned pair construction accepted.** The Broker may condition deterministic calibration/transfer scheduling on the already-frozen inferred partition, provided construction is canonical, systematic across proposed group/action combinations, and never cherry-picks outcomes.
4. **Inconsistent B cells are failures.** If calibration observations for the same inferred `(q_hat,a)` cell contain different observed outcomes, that cell is inconsistent. Every transfer prediction relying on that cell is deterministically scored incorrect. No majority vote, smoothing, rescue, or optimization is permitted.

## Mandatory missing link: empirical A gate

Before any benchmark authorization, the V1 pre-exposure freeze must specify exactly how the final A partition is empirically tested without a symbolic AST. It must also enforce the hard anti-memorization cardinality cap

`k_hat <= floor(sqrt(|E|))`.

For the frozen 16-entity geometry this means `k_hat <= 4`. A candidate may not assign one unique group per entity.

## Authorization boundary

Authorized now:

- exact deterministic B calibration/transfer construction;

The Tool-Assisted V0 termination remains binding and is not reopened by V1.
